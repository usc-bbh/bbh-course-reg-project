# Directing Minor

---

The Minor in Directing is a 20-unit minor that provides students with essential skills in theatrical storytelling, leadership and collaboration. Designed for those interested in the creative aspects of leadership and directing, the program offers a structured curriculum covering acting, directing fundamentals and leadership strategies. Through a combination of core course work and electives in dramaturgy, design and production, students gain hands-on experience in staging performances, working with actors and developing their artistic vision.

## Required Courses (16 units)

- THTR 305a Directing Units: 4
- THTR 305b Directing Units: 4
- THTR 451 Leadership Tools for the Director Units: 4

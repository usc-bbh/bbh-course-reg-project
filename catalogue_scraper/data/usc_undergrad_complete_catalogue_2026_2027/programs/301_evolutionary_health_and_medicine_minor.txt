# Evolutionary Health and Medicine Minor

---

The minor in Evolutionary Health and Medicine offers a rigorous exploration of human health through an evolutionary lens. It critically examines the historical constraints and evolutionary compromises inherent in the human body, utilizing these insights to elucidate vulnerabilities to disease. Through hands-on, project-based learning and potential collaborations with research professionals, students engage directly with the challenges of applying evolutionary thinking to modern health problems.

The minor requires at least 24 units.

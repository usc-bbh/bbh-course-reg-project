# Performing Social Change Minor

---

Performing social change brings the language and practice of Dramatic Arts into communities and events that seek to redress power imbalances, create healing and dialogue and develop new systems of interaction, communication, well-being and governance. This minor creates opportunities for exploration of social change through performance and theatrical practice.

- THTR 395m Drama as Human Relations Units: 4
- THTR 488mw Theatre in the Community Units: 4

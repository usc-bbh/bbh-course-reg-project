# Dramatic Arts, Directing Emphasis (BA)*

---

The BA in Dramatic Arts with an Emphasis in Directing provides students with the artistic, technical and leadership skills necessary to helm theatrical productions and collaborative creative projects. Combining rigorous training in script analysis, staging and actor direction with an exploration of dramatic literature and storytelling techniques, this program prepares students for careers in theater, film and emerging media. With hands-on experience in directing, project creation and interdisciplinary collaboration, graduates will be equipped to navigate the evolving landscape of the performing arts as innovative and versatile directors.

# Choreography for Stage and Screen Minor

---

The Choreography for Stage and Screen Minor explores the different techniques used to create dances for the stage and screen, including new media. By researching classic and contemporary film and stage musicals and existing choreographic works, combined with technical skills, students learn methods used to generate movement vocabulary and gain practical experience in choreographing for multiple mediums.

This minor consists of three lower-division courses, one upper-division course and 7 units of elective courses. Students must be in good academic standing to be admitted. No previous dance experience is required.

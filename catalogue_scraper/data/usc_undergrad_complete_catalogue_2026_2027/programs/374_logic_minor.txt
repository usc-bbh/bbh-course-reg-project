# Logic Minor

---

The interdisciplinary Logic minor requires a minimum of 18 units, at least 16 of which must be upper-division. Students must take at least 3 courses from the combined categories of Advanced Logic, and Upper-Division Logic, Math, and Philosophy.

## Logic Minor Requirements

---

A total of 18 units is required for the minor, at least 16 of which must be upper-division. In addition to the Foundational Logic requirement, students must complete at least three courses from the combined categories of Advanced Logic and Upper-Division Logic, Math, and Philosophy.

## Foundational Logic

---

Students must complete the Foundational Logic requirement. Students with sufficient background in Mathematics or Computer Science, or who have already taken PHIL 220 or PHIL 222g , may—with instructor permission and on a case-by-case basis—substitute this requirement with an additional Advanced Logic course.

- PHIL 350 Intermediate Logic Units: 4

## Advanced Logic

---

Students must complete at least one course from the following list.

- PHIL 450 The Limits of Logic Units: 4
- PHIL 451 Higher-order Logic Units: 4
- PHIL 452 Modal Logic Units: 4

## Upper-Division Logic, Math, and Philosophy

---

Students must complete courses from this category as part of the 18-unit minor requirement. At least three total courses must be taken from the combined categories of Advanced Logic and Upper-Division Logic, Math, and Philosophy.

- MATH 425a Fundamental Concepts of Analysis Units: 4
- MATH 430 Theory of Numbers Units: 4
- MATH 450 History of Mathematics Units: 4
- PHIL 381 Infinity in Mathematics and Philosophy Units: 4
- PHIL 480 Philosophy of Mathematics Units: 4

## Elective Courses

---

Students may select from the following courses to count toward the Logic minor.

- CSCI 102L Introduction to Programming Units: 2
- CSCI 103L Object Oriented Programming Units: 4
- CSCI 113x Programming Fundamentals for Computing Units: 4
- CSCI 114x Programming for Applications of Computing Units: 4
- CSCI 170 Discrete Methods in Computer Science Units: 4
- LING 303 Introduction to Semantics and Pragmatics Units: 4
- MATH 290 Mathematical Reasoning and Problem Solving Units: 4
- PHIL 220 Introduction to Logic Units: 4
- PHIL 222g Logic and Language Units: 4
- PHIL 258g Probability and Rational Choice Units: 4
- SLL 199 Chess and Critical Thinking Units: 2
- TAC 115 Programming in Python Units: 2

- TAC 165 Introduction to C++ Programming Units: 2

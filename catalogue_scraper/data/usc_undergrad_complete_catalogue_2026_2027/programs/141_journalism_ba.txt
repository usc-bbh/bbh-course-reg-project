# Journalism (BA)*

---

The undergraduate journalism degree at USC Annenberg School of Journalism teaches students to think critically and innovatively, to research, write and investigate, to create meaningful journalism across multiple platforms, and to understand the role of news and information in a democratic society. Students participate in multiple experiential learning opportunities through a modern Media Center newsroom, led by students and supported by faculty.

Students are required to complete 46 units for the major, including 32 units of required core courses.

## Required Courses, Lower Division

---

- JOUR 201 Culture of Journalism: Past, Present and Future Units: 4
- JOUR 206 Reporting and Writing Practicum Units: 1 (2 units required)
- JOUR 207 Reporting and Writing I Units: 4

## Required Courses, Upper Division

---

- JOUR 301 The Business of News Units: 2
- JOUR 307 Reporting and Writing II Units: 3
- JOUR 321 Visual Journalism Units: 3
- JOUR 322 Introduction to Investigative and Data Journalism Units: 2
- JOUR 372 Engaging Diverse Communities Units: 2
- JOUR 414 Advanced Digital Media Storytelling Units: 2
- JOUR 462 Law of Mass Communication Units: 4

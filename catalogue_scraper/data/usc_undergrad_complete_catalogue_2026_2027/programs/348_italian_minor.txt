# Italian Minor

---

The upper-division requirements include one core course plus an additional three courses to be selected in consultation with the department adviser (no more than two of which may be in English). Of the additional four courses, at least one must be at the 400 level.

Successful completion of the minor requires 32 units.

## Lower Division

---

Some or all of these courses may be waived by examination.

- ITAL 120 Italian I Units: 4
- ITAL 150 Italian II Units: 4
- ITAL 220 Italian III Units: 4
- ITAL 240 Italian IV Units: 4

## Upper Division (4 Courses)

---

### Required core course:

---

Students may choose either ITAL 300 or ITAL 350.

- ITAL 300g Inventing Modern Italy Units: 4 (in English) or
- ITAL 350g Gender and Sexuality in Renaissance Italy Units: 4 (in English)
- Students may take the Core course as early as their first semester of Italian and are strongly encouraged to complete it before enrolling in any 400-level course.

### Three upper-division Italian courses to be chosen from the following and to include at least one 400-level course:

---

No more than two courses conducted in English may be counted toward the minor.

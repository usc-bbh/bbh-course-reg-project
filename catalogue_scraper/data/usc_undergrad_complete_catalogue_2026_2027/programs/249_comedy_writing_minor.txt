# Comedy Writing Minor

---

Focused on the writing of comedy, students will learn to craft comedy across multiple platforms and refine their comedic voice. The curriculum is structured with three introduction courses: the study of comedy, the craft of comedic writing, and the importance of comedy in the human experience.

Students will be required to complete an original 4-unit long form project and then select 8 units of elective course work from a designated grouping of electives.

##### Application Procedures

To apply to the minor in comedy writing, a student must be in good academic standing and have a declared major.

Applications and information about the minor can be obtained from the USC School of Cinematic Arts, Writing for Screen and Television division, (213) 740-3303 or at writing@cinema.usc.edu subject line “Comedy Writing Minor Application.”

Students with a declared Comedy Writing minor will receive advisement support in the John Wells Division of Writing for Screen and Television and will receive priority d-clearance for non-majors.

##### Course Requirements for the Minor

A total of 20 units is required to complete the Comedy Writing Minor.

## Required Courses (8 units)

---

Foundations in structure, writing, and importance

- CTWR 404 Foundations of Comedy Units: 2
- CTWR 400 Is This A Joke: Mechanics of Comedy Writing Units: 4
- CTWR 406 Why The Long Face: Writing Comedy & the Human Experience Units: 2

## Long-Form Requirement (4 units From The Following)

---

Writing an original feature or television pilot with a comedy focus.

- CTWR 437 Writing the Original Situation Comedy Pilot Units: 4
- CTWR 457 Writing the Comedy Screenplay Units: 4

## Electives (8 units From The Following)

---

Please note repeatable courses.

- CTWR 324 Breaking the Half-Hour Television Series Units: 4
- CTWR 407 Creating the Comedic Character Units: 2
- CTWR 430 Comedy Writers and Their Work Units: 2 max 6
- CTWR 437 Writing the Original Situation Comedy Pilot Units: 4 max 8
- CTWR 438 Micro-Stories for New Platforms Units: 2, 4 max 8
- CTWR 457 Writing the Comedy Screenplay Units: 4 max 12
- CTWR 467 Comedy Punch Ups and Table Readings Units: 2 max 6

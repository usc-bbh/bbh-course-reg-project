# Law, Advocacy and Persuasive Performance Minor

---

The minor in Law, Advocacy and Persuasive Performance is an innovative and interdisciplinary minor that explores the intersection between dramatic arts and the law which emphasizes performance-based skills essential to the law and the legal profession: persuasive speaking, improvisation, audience analysis and storytelling.

The minor totals 16 unit: 8 required law and theatre units, 4 law and 4 dramatic arts elective units.

## Required Courses: 8 units

---

- LAW 200w Law and Society Units: 4
- THTR 122 Improvisation and Theatre Games Units: 2
- THTR 421 Public Speaking as Performance: A Course for Non-Actors Units: 2

## Law Electives: 4 units

---

- LAW 150w Stereotypes, Prejudice and the Rule of Law Units: 4
- LAW 202 Wrongful Convictions Units: 4
- LAW 203 Criminal Punishment in the Age of Mass Incarceration Units: 4
- LAW 212 Immigration Law for a New America Units: 4
- LAW 250w Children and the Law Units: 4
- LAW 301 Voting Rights and the Law Units: 4
- LAW 310w Global Justice for Mass Atrocities and Genocide Units: 4
- LAW 320p Law, Enslavement and Race Units: 4
- LAW 355 Law and Homelessness Units: 4
- LAW 403 Mental Health Law Units: 4

## Dramatic Arts Electives: 4 units

---

- THTR 101 Introduction to Acting Units: 4
- THTR 216 Movement for Actors Units: 2
- THTR 322 Improv 2: Advanced Improv Units: 2
- THTR 414 Secrets of Storytelling Units: 2
- THTR 426 Being on Screen: A Class for Presenters, Hosts, and Others Units: 2
- THTR 445 Developing Your Speaking Voice Units: 2
- THTR 479 Solo Performance Units: 4

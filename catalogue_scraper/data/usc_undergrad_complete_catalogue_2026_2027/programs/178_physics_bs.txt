# Physics (BS)

---

This program is intended primarily for students who are interested in a career in physics.

## Required Lower-Division Courses

---

- CHEM 105aLg General Chemistry Units: 4 *
- MATH 125g Calculus I Units: 4
- MATH 126g Calculus II Units: 4
- MATH 225 Linear Algebra and Linear Differential Equations Units: 4
- MATH 226g Calculus III Units: 4
- PHYS 161Lg Advanced Principles of Physics I Units: 4
- PHYS 162L Advanced Principles of Physics II Units: 4
- PHYS 163L Advanced Principles of Physics III Units: 4
- PHYS 190 Physics Discovery Series Units: 4

## Required Upper-Division Courses

---

- MATH 445 Mathematics of Physics and Engineering II Units: 4
- PHYS 304 Mechanics Units: 4
- PHYS 316 Thermodynamics and Statistical Mechanics Units: 4
- PHYS 408a Electricity and Magnetism Units: 4
- PHYS 408b Electricity and Magnetism Units: 4
- PHYS 438a Introduction to Quantum Mechanics and its Applications Units: 4
- PHYS 438b Introduction to Quantum Mechanics and its Applications Units: 4
- PHYS 440 Introduction to Condensed Matter Physics Units: 4 **
- PHYS 492L Senior Lab Units: 4
- PHYS 493L Advanced Experimental Techniques Units: 4

## Total units: 76

---

*CHEM 115aLg , CHEM 105bL , MATH 114xg , MATH 208x , or other 4-unit quantitative STEM course not already required may be substituted for CHEM 105aLg .
**Any 4-unit 400-level Physics course not already listed in the requirements may be substituted for PHYS 440 , with the exception of seminar and directed research courses.

For those wishing to pursue an honors degree, a senior thesis with at least 2 units of PHYS 490x is also required. Students who maintain a GPA of at least 3.5 in physics courses are eligible for graduation with honors, or high honors with a GPA of at least 3.8.

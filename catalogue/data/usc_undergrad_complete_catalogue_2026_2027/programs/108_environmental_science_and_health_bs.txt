# Environmental Science and Health (BS)

---

The Environmental Science and Health BS degree combines interdisciplinary courses on sustainability with traditional biology and chemistry content to provide options for students preparing for one of the health professions with an undergraduate emphasis on environmental sustainability. The Environmental Science and Health BS degree incorporates recommended preparation for medical school.

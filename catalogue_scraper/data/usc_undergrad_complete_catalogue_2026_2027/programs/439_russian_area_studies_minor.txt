# Russian Area Studies Minor

---

## Lower-division Requirements

---

One lower-division course from the courses detailed below or selected in consultation with an adviser:

- RUSS 120 Beginning Russian I Units: 4
- RUSS 140 Russian Language and Culture in the Post-Soviet Space Units: 2
- RUSS 150 Beginning Russian II Units: 4
- RUSS 220 Intermediate Russian I Units: 4
- RUSS 250 Intermediate Russian II Units: 4
- RUSS 255 Business Russian Units: 4
- SLL 110g Russia in the Modern Era Units: 4
- SLL 199 Chess and Critical Thinking Units: 2
- SLL 210 Masterpieces of the Russian Short Story Units: 4
- SLL 299 Chess — Advanced Thinking Techniques Units: 2

## Upper-division Requirements

---

### The core course

---

- SLL 330gp Russian Thought and Civilization Units: 4

### Students may take 4 units outside the Slavic department, from among the following:

---

- HIST 415 Medieval and Early Modern Russia Units: 4, 2 years
- HIST 416 History of Imperial Russia: 1689–1917 Units: 4
- HIST 417 History of Soviet Russia: 1917–1991 Units: 4, 2 years
- HIST 424 Family, Work, and Leisure in Russian History Units: 4
- IR 345 Russian and Post-Soviet Foreign Policy Units: 4
- IR 346 Communism and Post-Communism: Eastern Europe and the Balkans Units: 4
- IR 439 Political Economy of Russia and Eurasia Units: 4
- MDA 333 Colloquium in Armenian Studies: Social and Cultural Issues Units: 2
- HIST 323 The Holocaust in 20th Century Europe Units: 4

### And a minimum of two electives, to be chosen from among: any upper-division SLL/RUSS course in Russian or Eastern European literature, art or culture

---

- SLL 300 The Russian Novel Units: 4
- SLL 301 Russian Literary Avant-Garde Units: 4
- SLL 302g Modern Russian Literature Units: 4
- SLL 303 Contemporary Russian Literature Units: 4
- SLL 310gw The Eastern European Jewish Experience: Literature and Film Units: 4
- SLL 321 Cultures of Russia Units: 4
- SLL 344g Tolstoy: Writer and Moralist Units: 4
- SLL 345g Literature and Philosophy: Dostoevsky Units: 4
- SLL 346 Russian Drama and the Western Tradition Units: 4
- SLL 348g The Novels of Vladimir Nabokov Units: 4
- SLL 378 Modern Russian Art Units: 4
- SLL 397p Literature, Ideology and Film in Eastern European History Units: 4

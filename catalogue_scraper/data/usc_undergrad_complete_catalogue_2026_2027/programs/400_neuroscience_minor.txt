# Neuroscience Minor

---

Coordinator: David McKemy, PhD
Undergraduate Advisers: Pablo Lopez, pablol@usc.edu; Mia Kirsten Santos, mnsantos@usc.edu

#### Grade Requirements

A grade of C- or higher is required to count toward minor requirements.

### Note:

---

*An equivalent course may be substituted with permission.

**Prerequisite: BISC 220 or BISC 221 .

***Prerequisite: PSYC 100 .

Recommended but not required: CHEM 103Lx (or CHEM 105La ) plus MATH 125

Students who have not already taken the prerequisites (BISC 220 /BISC 221 and PSYC 100 ) will need to take a total of 28 units to satisfy the requirements of a minor in neuroscience. Thus, the range of units will vary from 20 to 28 depending on a student’s background; consult an academic adviser.

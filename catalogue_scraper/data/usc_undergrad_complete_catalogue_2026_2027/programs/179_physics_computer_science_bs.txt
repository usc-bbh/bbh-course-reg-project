# Physics/Computer Science (BS)

---

This program is intended for students with dual interests in physics and computer science who wish to complete the essential courses for both majors within their normal four-year career.

## Required Lower-Division Courses

---

- CSCI 103L Object Oriented Programming Units: 4
- CSCI 104L Data Structures Units: 4
- CSCI 170 Discrete Methods in Computer Science Units: 4
- CSCI 201 Principles of Software Development Units: 4
- CSCI 270 Introduction to Algorithms and Theory of Computing Units: 4
- MATH 125g Calculus I Units: 4
- MATH 126g Calculus II Units: 4
- MATH 225 Linear Algebra and Linear Differential Equations Units: 4
- MATH 226g Calculus III Units: 4
- PHYS 161Lg Advanced Principles of Physics I Units: 4
- PHYS 162L Advanced Principles of Physics II Units: 4
- PHYS 163L Advanced Principles of Physics III Units: 4
- PHYS 190 Physics Discovery Series Units: 4

## Required Upper-Division Courses

---

- CSCI 350 Fundamentals of Operating Systems Units: 4
- CSCI 356 Computer Systems Units: 4
- MATH 445 Mathematics of Physics and Engineering II Units: 4
- PHYS 304 Mechanics Units: 4
- PHYS 408a Electricity and Magnetism Units: 4
- PHYS 408b Electricity and Magnetism Units: 4
- PHYS 438a Introduction to Quantum Mechanics and its Applications Units: 4

## Total units: 80

---

For those wishing to pursue an honors degree, a senior thesis with at least 2 units of PHYS 495 is also required. Students who maintain a GPA of at least 3.5 in physics courses are eligible for graduation with honors, or high honors with a GPA of at least 3.8.

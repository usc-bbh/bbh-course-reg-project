# Russian Minor

---

Lower-division requirements for the major (RUSS 120 , RUSS 150 , RUSS 220 and choice of RUSS 250 or RUSS 255 ) plus three elective courses chosen from the following (at least two of the areas must be represented): Russian language (RUSS 310 , RUSS 340 ); Russian literature and culture taught in Russian (SLL 321 , RUSS 465 ); Russian literature, art and culture taught in translation (SLL 110g , SLL 330gp , SLL 344g , SLL 345g , SLL 348g , SLL 378 ); and translation (MDA 305 ).

Minimum units required: 28

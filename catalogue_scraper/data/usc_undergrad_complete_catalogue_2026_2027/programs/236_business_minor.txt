# Business Minor

---

The minor in business is available to students in all schools and departments except the Marshall School of Business. The minor provides the opportunity for students to gain understanding of key concepts and tools of business.

To enroll in the business minor, students must have completed a minimum of 32 units of college-level courses and attained a minimum overall GPA of 2.75.

Successful completion of the business minor requires at least 23 units with a minimum cumulative GPA of 2.0 in the courses applied to the minor.

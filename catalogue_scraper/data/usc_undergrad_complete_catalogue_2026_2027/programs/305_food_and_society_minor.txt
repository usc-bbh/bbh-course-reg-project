# Food and Society Minor

---

The interdisciplinary minor in Food and Society explores food as a complex social phenomenon, shaped by human knowledges, practices and ecologies. Grounded in Anthropology, the minor in Food and Society allows students to examine the diversity that characterizes human relationships to food, and to apply the tools of social analysis to gain critical insight into the complex social forces that shape human food systems and foodways.

## Core Requirement (4 units):

---

- ANTH 338g Food in Culture and Society Units: 4

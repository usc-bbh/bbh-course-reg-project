# Economics (BA)

---

Students are required to take ECON 203 , ECON 205 , ECON 303 , ECON 305 , ECON 317 , ECON 318 and four economics elective courses. Of the four elective courses (300 level or above) a minimum of two must be economics courses at the 400 level or higher. The remaining two economics courses must be approved by the department’s director of undergraduate studies. A grade of C (2.0) or better is required for each of the core courses ECON 303 , ECON 305 , ECON 317 and ECON 318 . MATH 118gx or MATH 125 is required for the major; students are advised to meet the requirement by their sophomore year. Majors are also required to take at least one two-unit course on computing chosen from TAC 101 or CSCI 101L.

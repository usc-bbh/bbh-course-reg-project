# Applied AI in Business Minor

---

This minor helps students in any profeessional path develop practical, career-ready AI skills and shape how these technologies are used in the real world.

Students develop the ability to recognize where AI can create genuine value, design AI-enabled solutions to complex problems, and communicate insights to inform decision-making. They learn how to lead AI adoption in ways that align with organizational and personal values, while maintaining stakeholder trust. The curriculum emphasizes applied learning, encouraging students to engage with AI as a tool for solving real challenges across industries. Graduates will be able to contribute immediately in AI-enabled environments and to guide the thoughtful adoption of emerging technologies over time.

##### Requirements for Admission

To declare this minor, students must have completed a minimum of 32 units of college-level course work and have a minimum overall GPA of 2.75.

##### Degree Requirements

Successful completion of the Applied AI in Business minor requires 16 units with a minimum combined GPA of 2.0 in the courses applied to the minor.

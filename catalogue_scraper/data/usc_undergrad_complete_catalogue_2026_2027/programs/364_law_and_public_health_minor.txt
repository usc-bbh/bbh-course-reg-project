# Law and Public Health Minor

---

The Minor in Law and Public Health offers an interdisciplinary foundation for understanding how legal systems shape health services, health behaviors, and population health outcomes. Students examine domestic and global health law, ethics, regulation, and health systems, with particular attention to health equity, patient rights, and the social determinants of health. The minor equips students with analytical tools to critically assess how law can both promote and hinder public health, preparing them for careers in health, law, policy, research, and advocacy across clinical, institutional, and societal settings.

The minor totals 20 units; 8 units of required courses, a minimum of 4 or maximum of 8 units of health promotion and disease prevention electives and a minimum of 4 or maximum of 8 units LAW electives.

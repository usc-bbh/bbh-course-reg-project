# Coaching, Health and Wellness Minor

---

The minor in Coaching, Health and Wellness provides students with a foundation in exercise science, athletic coaching and principles of health and wellness. This interdisciplinary program equips students with the knowledge and skills to promote performance, physical fitness, mental well-being and overall health in diverse settings. Whether students aspire to work with athletes, are fitness enthusiasts or seek to improve their wellness, this minor offers a framework for those passionate about fostering healthy lifestyles and peak performance.

## Coaching Core

---

Choose one coaching course:

- PHED 162 Principles of Athletic Coaching Units: 2 or
- PHED 163 Health Coaching Units: 3

## Note:

---

A course used to satisfy the coaching requirement cannot be double counted to satisfy an elective requirement. No more than 4 units of PHED “activity” courses may be applied to a student’s overall unit requirement; consult an adviser about course selection.

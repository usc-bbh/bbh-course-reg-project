# Business Administration (Cinematic Arts) (BS)

---

This program consists of courses offered by both the Marshall School and the School of Cinematic Arts. Students completing the program receive a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration with an emphasis in Cinematic Arts. The program is available to entering freshmen only.

#### Requirements for Completion

To complete the program, students must satisfy all requirements for the Bachelor of Science degree in business, including USC’s General Education requirements, as well as an additional 24 units in cinematic arts, which specifically address the business side of the industry.

## Composition/Writing Requirements (8 units)

- WRIT 340 Advanced Writing and Critical Engagement Units: 4 *
- * Note: A section of WRIT 340 titled “Advanced Writing for Business” is recommended for BCT majors.

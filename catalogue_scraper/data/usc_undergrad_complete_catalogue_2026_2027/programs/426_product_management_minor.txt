# Product Management Minor

---

The Product Management minor provides specialized skill sets for students that they will apply to succeed in the workplace, regardless of their chosen profession. Students will learn to apply product leadership frameworks and utilize rapidly evolving product management tools and technologies, to facilitate effective processes among organizational stakeholders and cross-functional product teams.

The minor signals to potential employers that the graduate is ready to lead and motivate teams in-person and virtually, facilitate complex decision-making among diverse groups and individuals across cultures, and guide the development, launch, and scaling of successful products and solutions to support the success of the organization. Students will gain exposure to the full product lifecycle including identifying product-market fit, leveraging data for strategic decisions, and managing stakeholder relationships to deliver meaningful business outcomes.

Requirements for Admission

To declare this minor, students must have completed a minimum of 32 units of college-level course work and have a minimum overall GPA of 2.75.

Successful completion of the Product Management Minor requires minimum GPA of 2.0 for the 16 units applied to the program.

## Required Courses

---

- Complete both of the following courses:
- MOR 452 Product Management I - Essentials and Frameworks Units: 2
- MOR 453 Product Management II - Execution and Alignment Units: 2
- Complete one of the following courses focused on understanding the consumer:
- BAEP 450 Fundamentals of Entrepreneurship Units: 4
- MKT 445 New Product Development and Branding Units: 4
- Complete one of the following courses focused on applications:
- BAEP 452 Feasibility Analysis Units: 4
- DSO 433 Designing Digital Processes and User Experience with AI Units: 4
- MKT 451 User Experience (UX) and Design for Marketing Units: 4
- MOR 454 Applied Product Management and Leadership Units: 4

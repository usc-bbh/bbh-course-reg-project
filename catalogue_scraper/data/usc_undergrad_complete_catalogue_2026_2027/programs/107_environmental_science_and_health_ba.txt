# Environmental Science and Health (BA)

---

The Environmental Science and Health BA degree combines interdisciplinary courses on sustainability with traditional biology and chemistry content to provide options for students preparing for one of the health professions with an undergraduate emphasis on environmental sustainability. The Environmental Science and Health BA degree may be appropriate for students preparing for other graduate or professional training as well as students pursuing double majors.

# Psychiatry and Behavioral Sciences Minor

---

The Psychiatry and Behavioral Sciences minor offers students the ability to explore knowledge, concepts and research in the areas of psychiatry and behavioral health, providing a solid foundation of these salient topics for students interested in pursuing professions within the healthcare field or applying these concepts more broadly in professional settings.

A minimum of 16 units is required.

## Requirements

---

Choose four of the following courses:

- PBHS 301g Science and Practice of Building Resilience Units: 4
- PBHS 302 The Community Impact of Historical Trauma Units: 4
- PBHS 303 Adverse Childhood Experiences and Trauma Units: 4
- PBHS 304 Mental Health and Climate Change Units: 4
- PBHS 305 The Science of Altruism Units: 4
- PBHS 306 Team-Based Care: Roles of Mental Health Professionals Units: 4
- PBHS 307 Dynamics of Power and Gender-Based Violence Units: 4
- PBHS 310w Identity Development of the Contemporary Male Units: 4

## Minimum Required Units: 16

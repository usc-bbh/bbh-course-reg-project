# Sociology Minor

---

Five courses (20 units) are required to complete the minor in sociology.

All minors are required to take at least two of the core courses in sociology:

## Core Courses

---

- SOCI 200gm Introduction to Sociology Units: 4
- or
- SOCI 270g Sociological Theory Units: 4
- SOCI 313L Sociological Research Methods Units: 4
- or
- SOCI 314Lg Analyzing Social Statistics Units: 4

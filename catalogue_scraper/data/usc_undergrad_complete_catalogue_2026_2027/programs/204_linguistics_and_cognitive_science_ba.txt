# Linguistics and Cognitive Science (BA)

---

## Capstone Experience

---

The Linguistics and Cognitive Science major requires a capstone experience, which can be fulfilled in one of the following ways:

(A) A research paper completed as Honors Thesis LING 497 , or as Directed Research LING 490 or as a Linguistic 400-level course research paper.

(B) A poster presented at the USC UG symposium [https://ahf.usc.edu/signature-events/symposium/] or at a conference.

(C) A summer internship related to the degree.

## Linguistics and Cognitive Science Major with Honors

---

The Linguistics and Cognitive Science major with honors requires the student to complete the requirements for the major with a GPA of 3.5 or above and to complete LING 497 Honors Thesis with a grade of B+ or better. Intent to complete the major with honors should be registered with the undergraduate adviser no later than the second semester of the junior year.

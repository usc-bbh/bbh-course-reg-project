# Health Administration Minor

---

This 16-unit minor in health administration provides students with a background in administration and management issues in the field of health care and the skills necessary to pursue health-related management opportunities in the for-profit, nonprofit and governmental sectors.

## Required Courses (12 Units)

---

- PPD 202 Health and Health Care in America Today Units: 2 and
- PPD 205 Introduction to Health Policy and Law Units: 2
- or
- PPD 330 Introduction to Health Care Systems Units: 4
-
- PPD 325 Fundamentals of Health Policy and Management Units: 4
- PPD 413 Management of Health Care Organizations Units: 4

## Electives (4 units)

---

- ACAD 260 Introduction to Healthcare Innovation Units: 4
- MEDS 220 Healthcare Foundations: Core Concepts and Terminology Units: 2
- MEDS 405 Health Care Systems: A Doctor’s Perspective Units: 2
- PPD 320 Organizational Behavior for Public Leadership Units: 4
- PPD 407 Financial Management of Public and Nonprofit Organizations Units: 4
- PPD 414 Community Health Policy and Planning Units: 4

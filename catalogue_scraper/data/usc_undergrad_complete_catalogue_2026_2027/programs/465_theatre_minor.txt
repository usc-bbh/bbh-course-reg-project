# Theatre Minor

---

The general minor in theatre invites students to explore the many facets of this exciting field. Students have the opportunity to take a variety of classes in acting, playwriting, literature, stage management, directing, costume design and production. The curriculum is flexible and encourages students explore theatre through a broad combination of introductory and upper-division course work. All minor students are also eligible to participate in performance and production projects.

## Required Courses

---

- THTR 101 Introduction to Acting Units: 4
- THTR 125 Text Studies for Production Units: 4
- THTR 130 Introduction to Theatrical Production Units: 2

### One of the following:

---

- THTR 225g Theatre Across History and Cultures Units: 4
- THTR 230 Theatrical Design as Poetic Realism Units: 3
- THTR 231 Costume Construction Units: 3
- THTR 233 Historic Costumes for the Theatre Units: 3
- THTR 252a Intermediate Acting I: Drama/Comedy Units: 4
- THTR 305a Directing Units: 4
- THTR 333 Stage Management I Units: 3
- THTR 336 Sound Design I Units: 3
- THTR 365 Playwriting I Units: 4

# Video Game Production Minor

---

Video games have become the largest category in the entertainment industry. This minor will introduce you to techniques needed to manage this creative endeavor as a successful business enterprise and prepare you for a career in video game production.

## Required Courses (10 units)

---

### Complete the following (8 units)

---

- TAC 180 Video Game Production Units: 2
- TAC 181 Video Game QA Management Units: 2
- TAC 393 Video Game Project Management Units: 4

### Choose one of the following (2 units)

---

- CTIN 409 Producing Interactive Projects Units: 2
- or
- TAC 409 Producing Interactive Media Projects Units: 2

- BUAD 315x Basics of Project and Operations Management for Non-Majors Units: 2
- COMM 320 Small Group and Team Communication Units: 4

# Law and Government Minor

---

The Minor in Law and Government introduces the legal foundations of legal institutions and explores the intersections of law with government and politics.

The minor totals 16 units: 4 required core law units, at least 4 elective law units, and up to 8 units of electives from various departments as listed below.

## Required Core Courses (4 units)

---

- LAW 201w Law and Politics Units: 4

## LAW Electives (a minimum of 4 or maximum of 8 units)

---

- LAW 200w Law and Society Units: 4
- LAW 207 Personal Data in the 21st Century Units: 4
- LAW 210p Fundamentals of the U.S. Legal System Units: 4
- LAW 225 Current Court Cases Units: 2
- LAW 301 Voting Rights and the Law Units: 4
- LAW 305 International Influence: Law and Power Units: 4
- LAW 406 Individual Rights in U.S. Constitutional Law Units: 4
- LAW 407 Regulatory Law Units: 4
- LAW 498 Law Internship Units: 1, 2, 3, 4

## Other Electives (a minimum of 4 or maximum of 8 units)

---

- IR 309 Global Governance Units: 4
- POSC 335 Political Parties, Campaigns, and Elections Units: 4
- POSC 360 Comparative Political Institutions Units: 4
- POSC 395 Directed Governmental and Political Leadership Internship Units: 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8
- POSC 425 Legislative Process Units: 4
- POSC 499 Special Topics Units: 2, 3, 4
- PPD 240g Citizenship and Public Ethics Units: 4
- PPD 314 Public Policy and Law Units: 4
- PPD 342 Crime and Public Policy Units: 4
- PPD 357 Government and Business Units: 4
- SCDC 435 How Washington Really Works Units: 4

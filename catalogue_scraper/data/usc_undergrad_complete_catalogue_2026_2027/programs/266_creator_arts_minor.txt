# Creator Arts Minor

---

The Creator Arts minor within the School of Dramatic Arts prepares students to thrive in today’s rapidly evolving creator economy by uniting artistic practice with entrepreneurial strategy. Designed for students across disciplines, the minor equips undergraduates with the creative, strategic, and collaborative tools necessary to build sustainable, self-generated careers. Through hands-on, process-based course work, students develop their authentic creative voice while gaining fluency in branding, digital production, audience engagement, and creator-led business models. This 16-unit minor offers an interdisciplinary pathway for students who aspire to become multi-hyphenate artists, innovators, and creative leaders.

# Sports Law Minor

---

The Minor in Sports Law introduces the legal foundations of the sports law industry and explores regulation, contract drafting, dealmaking, and legal issues challenging this industry.

The Minor in Sports Law totals 16 units: 8 required core Law units, and 8 units of electives from Law and various departments as listed below.

## Required Core Courses (8 units)

---

- LAW 200w Law and Society Units: 4
- OR
- LAW 210p Fundamentals of the U.S. Legal System Units: 4
- AND
- LAW 324 Sports Law Units: 4

## Electives (8 units)

---

- COMM 381 Issues in Contemporary Sport Units: 4
- COMM 383m Sports, Communication and Culture Units: 4
- COMM 387 Sports and Social Change Units: 4
- JOUR 380 Sports, Business and Media in Today’s Society Units: 4
- JOUR 478 Politics of Sports Writing Units: 4
- JOUR 485 Sports Investigative Reporting Units: 4
- LAW 326 Law and the Modern Athlete Units: 4
- LAW 311 Fundamentals of Entertainment Law Units: 4
- LAW 350 Law and Entrepreneurship Units: 4
- OT 333 Sports Ethics Units: 4
